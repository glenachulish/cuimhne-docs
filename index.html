Cuimhne

Gaelic for memory — roughly “KOO-in-yuh”.

This is a way to record your life in whatever way you choose. Use words, photos, videos, recordings, web links.

Reflect on your life, dig up memories, reconnect with the years that have passed. You may also use this to reflect on how you are using your time now. Are you doing the things that matter most to you?

You can record periods of your life — school, jobs, places you've lived. This is the background to the daily events that you might record.

You can share your diary with other people electronically or print it as a PDF. This could be a good way for older people to connect with younger people.
